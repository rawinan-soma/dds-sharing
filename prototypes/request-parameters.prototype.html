DDS Sharing — what a Request stores, and what a Re-run refetches

The question this demo exists to answer: a Request never stores the ask a Requester made. It stores an expansion of it — the Report codes their Disease group meant at the moment they pressed submit, and the provinces their health region meant. Both classifications are amendable, and the dates the human typed are inclusive while upstream's are not. Driving submit, an announcement, a Decision and a Re-run by hand — does the stored Request still say what it meant, and does it still make a legal upstream call?

Fill in the form and press the buttons in any order, or run one of the six guided walkthroughs below. Nothing is saved; reload and it is gone. The sibling prototype request-lifecycle.prototype.html drives everything that happens after the extraction — Alerts, tokens and clocks. This one stops at the upstream call.

The classification starts as docs/disease-groups.md of 2026-09-02 and the geography as docs/provinces.csv. Both can be amended from the buttons, mid-flight, exactly as an announcement would amend them.
