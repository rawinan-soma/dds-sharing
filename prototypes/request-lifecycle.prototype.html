DDS Sharing — the Request lifecycle

The question this demo exists to answer: a Request passes through a human gate, a background extraction, an email nobody can confirm arrived, and three clocks that run at different speeds. Driving it by hand — can a Reviewer reach a state where the queue lies to them, or where an Alert can never be cleared?

Press the buttons in any order, or run one of the five guided walkthroughs below. Everything you see is derived from the event trail at the bottom of the page, exactly as the specification says the Request's state is a projection of its events. Nothing is saved; reload and it is gone.

Two Reviewers, Suda and Pichai, stand in for the real ones. Times are ICT. The business-hours clock is Mon–Fri 08:30–16:30, so a Saturday advances the wall clock and not the business one.
