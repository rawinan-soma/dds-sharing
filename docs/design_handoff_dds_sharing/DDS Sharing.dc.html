Request a data extract

Every request is read and approved by a named officer before any data is fetched. Approval takes up to 24 business hours, and the extract arrives by email — never on this page.

What you are asking for is de-identified, case-level surveillance records for one disease group, one date range and one optional area.

If a request is not approved you will be told that it was not approved, but not why. Please supply a telephone number you answer — a reviewer who cannot place you will call before deciding.

Before you start

What the extract contains

the same 23 columns for every request

Nothing in this extract names a person. A file with no names in it is the correct result, not a broken one. The columns are fixed, identical for every request, and never widened for an individual ask.

You will get

  • One row per case, one CSV file.
  • Disease — the report code the row was fetched under, the ICD-10 diagnosis and the full diagnosis list.
  • Person — birth date, age in completed years at onset, gender, prefix, nationality, occupation, marital status.
  • Where — province and district of registration, province and district recorded at survey, and the health region of the survey province.
  • Facility — the hospital code.
  • When — onset, treatment, diagnosis, death, report and last update.

You will not get

  • Names, surnames or national ID numbers.
  • Addresses, house numbers, roads or villages.
  • Sub-district (ตำบล) or anything finer — the district is the finest named geography.
  • Map coordinates of any kind.
  • Free text of any kind — every field is a code or a date.
  • Telephone numbers or any other contact detail of a case.

A field reaches the extract only by being on that list. It is not negotiable per request, and a reviewer cannot widen it for you. Every archive carries a Thai/English data dictionary naming each column.

The email address you give

The extract link goes to the email address below, and nowhere else. It is never checked — a typo sends the link to the wrong place, or nowhere, and nobody will tell you. Read it back before you submit.

What is kept, and for how long

Your contact details and the record of this request are kept indefinitely, so that every release of data stays traceable to the person who asked and the officer who approved it. Your contact details can be removed on request once the request is finished — telephone 0 2590 4000 and quote your reference number.
