## What to build

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

The four emails the service sends still use the old visual system. This ticket restyles them to B, following `docs/design/system.md` "Screen 9: the Requester's emails" and "Screen 9: the Reviewer's emails", and their frames on the Lunagraph page "Requester form: prototypes" in project `dds-prototype` (`9 · Email · Queue notification`, `9 · Email · Extraction failure`, and the Requester delivery and rejection frames). **Where `system.md` and a frame disagree, the frame wins.**

All four share one shell: a 680px body under a from / to / subject header over a hairline.

- **Delivery** (Requester): greeting, the approval line, a `primary` ดาวน์โหลดไฟล์ button, the ask in a bordered box, the 72-hour terms, and the zip note with *do not forward* in `pending` on `pending-wash`. **No row count.**
- **Rejection** (Requester): greeting, the outcome on an `inert-wash` panel (calm, not red), no reason, the phone line.
- **Queue notification** (every Reviewer): the Requester's name, workplace and decision deadline in a bordered box, then that the email carries no patient data and no download link.
- **Extraction failure** (the approving Reviewer): the failure on a `pending-wash` panel; that the Requester has received nothing and will only know when the Reviewer calls; that the operator was told separately and the Reviewer need not fix anything; that the Alert waits under ต้องจัดการ.

Email HTML is not the app: B's token values are inlined as literal colours, with table-based layout and inline styles as email clients require. The download link keeps its fixed `/d/<token>` path (ADR 0018). Content, recipients and send triggers do not change beyond what the four bullets above state.
