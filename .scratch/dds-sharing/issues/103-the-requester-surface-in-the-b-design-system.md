# The Requester surface in the B design system

Status: ready-for-agent
Labels: enhancement

## What to build

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

The design was replaced on 2026-09-30 by Requester-form variant B (blue accent, rounded white cards with one low shadow on a cool grey page, green for "done", the map as the largest thing on the Requester's screen) and re-locked after the design critique the same day. The app still renders the old square, flat, teal "surveillance map" system everywhere. This ticket moves the app onto the B tokens and rebuilds every Requester screen in B. The Reviewer surface (#104) and the emails (#105) follow and depend on this.

**The contract is `docs/design/system.md`**: its Tokens section, "Layout: the map-first split", "The Requester screens, as locked", and every Component section a Requester screen uses (Button, Card, Field, Date field, Field tooltip, Consent block, Region map, Segmented, Requirement checklist, Tag, Chip). Behaviour, states, edge cases and copy in `docs/design/handoff.md` still stand where `system.md` does not supersede them; `docs/design/accessibility.md` holds the contrast and focus rules. **Where `system.md` and a frame disagree, the frame wins.** The frames are on the Lunagraph page "Requester form: prototypes" in project `dds-prototype`, readable through the Lunagraph MCP. Build from the numbered frames (1, 1m, 1b, 2, 3, 7, 7m, 8 and their states). The frame `B · Map-first split` is the reference the tokens were taken from, **not a screen**: it has no de-identification block, no consent and no calendar button, and must not be built.

**The tokens.** The global stylesheet's custom properties become the B tokens from `docs/design/source/globals.css` / `system.md`, written as plain CSS custom properties as today (the Tailwind `@theme` in `source/` is design-tool scaffolding, not the implementation). Old token names that B removed (`land`, `border-strong`, `ready` and the like) are gone, not aliased. Because the tokens are global, the Reviewer surface will change colour with this ticket; it must keep working and stay legible, but restyling it is #104. Font weight 500 is loaded alongside 400 and 600, still self-hosted (no request leaves the ministry network). If Angular renders border colours correctly without it, the canvas-fault workaround block described in `system.md` ("A canvas rendering fault, still guarded") is not carried over.

**The date field.** Dates are typed and shown in พ.ศ., never a native date input, with a calendar button that opens a พ.ศ. calendar popover which cannot offer a span over 365 days (`system.md` "Date field"). The span cap and the existing validation keep their behaviour.

**The prototype goes.** The dev-only `?variant=` switch in the app routes and the Requester-form prototype folder it loads are deleted, along with anything only they use.

**Copy** stays in the Paraglide catalogue, authored in English (ADR 0010); the Thai on the canvas is a layout proxy except Disease group names, province names and เขตสุขภาพ numbering. New or changed strings are added as English catalogue keys with a Thai value; nothing is hard-coded in templates.

**The spec follows the canvas.** `system.md` Screen 9 notes that spec §16.4's order (gate notice → de-identification → notices → form), the de-identification block's *no names is the correct result* line and 1b's *address cannot be changed* warning no longer match the locked screens. Amend `docs/spec.md` (and `docs/design/brief.md` where it states the same) to the locked screens, and update the "Still undone" list in `system.md` for what this ticket finishes.

- [ ] Screen 1 is the map-first split: a sticky 360px map pane (eyebrow, area headline, the two mode segments, region map, caption in province mode, province chips) beside a 460px form card, stacking map-first below 900px with 52 × 42px map cells

- [ ] Every date field is typed and displayed in พ.ศ., has a calendar button, and its popover never offers a range over 365 days; no native date input remains
