## What to build

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

After #103 the app runs on the B tokens and the Requester surface is rebuilt, but the Reviewer screens still have the old square layout, only recoloured. This ticket rebuilds every Reviewer screen in B.

**The contract is `docs/design/system.md` "The Reviewer screens, as locked"** and the Component sections the Reviewer uses (Dialog, Toast, Statement, Empty state, Button, Card, Field, Tag, Chip). Behaviour, states, edge cases and copy in `docs/design/handoff.md` still stand where `system.md` does not supersede them. **Where `system.md` and a frame disagree, the frame wins.** The frames are on the Lunagraph page "Requester form: prototypes" in project `dds-prototype`, readable through the Lunagraph MCP: 4a–c (sign in), 5 (queue and dossier), 11a–f (the decision states), 6a–b (Alert and in-flight zones), 12a–d (empty queues and session warnings), 13 (lookup), 14a–b (loading) and R (below 1024px).

**The layout.** The queue sits above, as a table under three zone tabs; the dossier sits below it in two columns; the decision strip runs beneath both. The Reviewer header is no longer dark.

**"Where the drawing is not the build"** in `system.md` is binding: ไม่อนุมัติ and ↻ รีเฟรช are `Button secondary` on the `input` edge; buttons are `lg` (44px); the ไม่อนุมัติ / อนุมัติ pair keeps its 180 / 260px widths; ↻ and ▸ are line icons from the app's icon set, not text glyphs; tabs use `radius-sm`; the queue rows are a real `<table>` with row headers, each row a button or link to its dossier.

**The critique amendments.** The Alert card (6a) carries the Requester's contact details above its outcome buttons, because the outcome is a phone call. The ADR 0017 stated absence (6b, *the Requester's email address cannot be corrected*) is inert with a lock icon, not styled as failed. The approve confirm (11a, 14b) does not name the Reviewer, by decision.

**Copy** stays in the Paraglide catalogue, authored in English (ADR 0010); new or changed strings are English keys with a Thai value.
